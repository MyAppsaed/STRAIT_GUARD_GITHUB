Privacy Policy

STRAIT-GUARD · ClickTech · Last updated: 3 August 2026

Overview

STRAIT-GUARD is a fully offline single-player game. It does not require registration or a user account, and does not directly collect any personal information from you.

Advertising

The game displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect limited advertising data (such as device identifier, approximate location, and app usage) in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

No gameplay data is uploaded to our servers. High scores, upgrades, and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact ClickTech at saedserv@gmail.com.
